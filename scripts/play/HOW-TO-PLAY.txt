EVOLUTION - how to play
=======================

Windows:  double-click PLAY.bat
Mac:      double-click play.command
          (the first time, right-click it > Open, because Mac is careful
           with downloaded files)
Linux:    run ./play.command

A small black window opens and the game opens in your web browser.
Keep the black window open while you play; close it to stop the game.

It needs Node.js (free, from https://nodejs.org), or Python. If neither
is installed, the window says so.

Saves are kept in the browser on this computer.
Grown-ups: add ?unlock=all to the end of the address (for example
http://localhost:8173/?unlock=all) for check mode: every chapter
unlocked, with Skip and Jump buttons. Without it the game plays normally.
